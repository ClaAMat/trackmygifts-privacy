Privacy Policy for TrackMyGifts

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

Overview

TrackMyGifts is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the TrackMyGifts mobile application.

Data Collection and Storage

TrackMyGifts does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data, usage analytics, or device identifiers on external servers. All information you enter into the app—including gift lists, recipient names, notes, and budgets—is stored strictly locally on your device. Because we do not collect any data, we do not sell, share, or disclose your information to third parties.

Third-Party Services and Tracking

TrackMyGifts does not integrate any third-party advertising networks, analytics tools, or tracking SDKs.

Children's Privacy

Because TrackMyGifts does not collect any personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if new features are added to the app. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
